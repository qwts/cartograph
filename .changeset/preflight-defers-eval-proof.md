Preflight no longer runs the full TypeScript parse, which roughly halves the time from connecting a large repository to its first graph. Inline `eval()` and `new Function()` sites are listed as pending until recovery proves them, and recovery then closes, downgrades or keeps each one exactly as before. After a local recovery the Preflight view shows that final classification. Running preflight again lists the sites as pending until the next recovery.
